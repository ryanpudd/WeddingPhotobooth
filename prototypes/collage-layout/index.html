How should collage lay out N photos in the window?

A real collage made from the test photos with the crop rule from prototype/crop-rule (same detections, same placement). Every tile is cropped at one shared zoom to the tile's own aspect. Move the controls, or click a row in the grid table to force that grid (--grid CxR). The questions are how the automatic grid is chosen, the gap width, how far tiles may stray from the photo's 16:9, and whether the ~3840px cap holds up. "Render full size" opens the composite at its true output size.
